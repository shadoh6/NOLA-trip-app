# NOLA Trip Picks

A family voting app for our Houston → New Orleans trip (Nov 21–27).

Open the site, pick your name, and vote on each attraction (Want / Maybe / Skip, plus Must-do and notes). Votes are saved on your own phone. When you're done, open **Results**, tap **Copy all votes**, and text it to Roger.
